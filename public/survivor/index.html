Every week, someone pays a contractor in USDC and gets ghosted. Someone else delivers work and spends weeks chasing payment. Both sides argue in Telegram DMs with screenshots as evidence. There is no neutral system. Until now.

How it works

01

Lock

Payer deposits USDC into a per-contract vault on Solana. Funds are isolated.

02

Work

Payee delivers against scope and deadline. Submit proof as you go.

03

Resolve

System evaluates proof: release, refund, or fair split. 48h challenge window.

Why Survivor

Per-contract vaults

Every payment isolated in its own PDA. No commingling risk.

Deterministic resolution

Weighted rules, not black-box AI. Every decision auditable.

On-chain enforcement

Outcomes execute as Solana transactions. Not promises.

Dead man's switch

If Survivor goes down, payer reclaims after 72h. Funds never stuck.
